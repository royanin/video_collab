Quick Start Guide:

1. Send room URL via email or messenger apps.

2. Say hi to others via the text chat. Launch video chat with CHAT 

3. Decide what to watch, paste the URL in the field "Copy paste Youtube URL...", and hit LOAD 
Only one person has to load the video.

4. Use WRITE  to show/hide your shared scratchpad. Insert the video timestamp in your notes with
Save your notes before leaving the room.
